# Good Example: Stakeholder Analysis

**Project:** Enterprise CRM Migration
**Date:** September 2026

## Stakeholder Roster & Alignment Matrix

### 1. David Vance
* **Department:** Global Sales
* **Role:** VP of Sales / Executive Sponsor
* **Power:** High (Controls project budget and final sign-off)
* **Interest:** High (Needs pipeline visibility but fears team downtime)
* **Analysis:** Supportive of the goal but highly protective of active selling hours. If implementation impacts end-of-quarter sales targets, his alignment will degrade quickly.
* **Engagement Plan:**
  * Conduct concise, bi-weekly 15-minute status syncs focused on milestone progress.
  * Coordinate all user training schedules through his chief of staff to strictly avoid the final two weeks of the fiscal quarter.

### 2. Elena Rostova
* **Department:** Cyber Security & Compliance
* **Role:** Director of Risk Management
* **Power:** High (Has veto authority over data governance structures)
* **Interest:** Medium (Focuses exclusively on regulatory compliance and data boundaries)
* **Analysis:** Neutral but highly risk-averse. Demands early, formal documentation regarding data residency laws and third-party vendor access controls.
* **Engagement Plan:**
  * Deliver the vendor's SOC 2 Type II report and data mapping schemas during the initial sprint.
  * Secure explicit sign-off on the security architecture before moving from sandbox testing to staging.

### 3. Customer Success Team
* **Department:** Customer Operations
* **Role:** Frontline End-Users (140+ staff)
* **Power:** Low (Individual contributors, but critical for cultural adoption)
* **Interest:** High (System workflow speed dictates their daily ticket handling capacity)
* **Analysis:** Skeptical. The team exhibits high anxiety regarding UI complexity and fears a temporary drop in productivity metrics due to a steep learning curve.
* **Engagement Plan:**
  * Embed two designated "CS Champions" into the weekly User Acceptance Testing (UAT) loop.
  * Launch an asynchronous Slack help channel for real-time friction reporting during the rollout phase.
