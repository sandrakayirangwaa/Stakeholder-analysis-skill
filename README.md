# Stakeholder Analysis Skill

This repository contains a stakeholder analysis skill for AI assistants. It helps you understand the people and groups involved in a project, what they care about, how the project may affect them, and where problems or resistance might come from.

The skill uses your project documents and stakeholder list to create a practical analysis that you can use when planning or introducing a new product, system, or change.

## What It Does

- Groups stakeholders based on their level of power and interest.
- Looks at how each stakeholder may be affected by the project.
- Identifies possible concerns, disagreements, or reasons a stakeholder might resist the change.
- Looks at what each stakeholder is likely to care about based on their role and responsibilities.
- Suggests how to communicate with and involve different stakeholders.
- Points out stakeholders who may have a major influence on whether the project succeeds.

## What It Won't Do

- It will not make important business decisions for you.
- It will not claim to know exactly what a stakeholder is thinking or feeling.
- It will not assume that everyone will support the project.
- It will not make up information about stakeholders when there is not enough information available.
- It will not write or send messages to stakeholders.

## How to Use It

1. Load `skill.md` into your AI assistant or prompt setup.
2. Provide your project documentation, such as a PRD, project brief, research report, or operational document.
3. Provide a good example of a stakeholder analysis to show the level of detail and quality you want.
4. Provide a bad example to show the types of shallow or unrealistic analysis that should be avoided.
5. Add your stakeholder list, including their names, departments, or roles.
6. Run the skill to generate the stakeholder analysis and engagement plan.

## Example Requests

You can ask the skill things like:

- "Which stakeholders are most important for this project, and why?"
- "Who is most likely to resist this change?"
- "What concerns might the operations team have about this project?"
- "Analyze these stakeholders and group them based on their power and interest."
- "What is the best way to involve the compliance team without slowing the project down?"
- "Review this project document and identify the main stakeholder risks."

## Files

- `skill.md` — Contains the instructions that define how the stakeholder analysis works.
- `README.md` — Explains what the skill does and how to use it.
